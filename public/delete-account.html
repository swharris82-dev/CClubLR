Delete your account

Ask the club to delete your Clubhouse IQ account. You can also do this in the app under My account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in
  • Your name, email, phone number, and alert settings

What stays with the club

Work orders, equipment records, inspections, and photos you added stay in the club's maintenance history. Your name can still appear where it was written into a record, such as a signature.

The administrator usually handles requests within 30 days. See the privacy policy for details.
