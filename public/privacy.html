Clubhouse IQ

Privacy policy

Clubhouse IQ is the facilities and maintenance app used by the Country Club of Little Rock. Effective November 22, 2026.

Who uses it

Club facilities staff, managers, and department staff the club invites. There is no public sign-up. Accounts are created by a club administrator.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, work email, role, and, if you add it, a cell number for text alerts. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash.
  • Work records: work orders, maintenance requests, equipment records, inspections, notes, signatures, and the photos you take or upload (for example before and after photos and equipment nameplates).
  • Camera and microphone: used only when you take a photo, scan a tag, or start a live video diagnosis. Live video and audio are sent to the AI service during the call and are not recorded. If you save the call, the written transcript and summary are saved to that equipment's history.
  • Building system readings: temperatures, pressures, and equipment status from the club's building automation system.
  • Basic usage: sign-in times and who created or changed a record, so work history is accurate.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising IDs. There are no ads and no third-party analytics.

How it is used

Only to run the club's maintenance program: tracking and assigning work, keeping equipment history, sending alerts you turn on, and producing reports for club management.

Services that process data for us

  • Render hosts the app and its database.
  • Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude receive the photos, text, and live video you send for AI diagnosis, nameplate scanning, and report summaries, and return a result.
  • Email and text message providers deliver the alerts you choose to receive.

We do not sell or share personal information for any other purpose.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Access is limited by role, and department staff only see their own requests.

How long we keep it

Work and equipment records are kept as part of the club's maintenance history for as long as the club uses Clubhouse IQ. Building system readings are kept for 90 days. Account details are kept until the account is deleted.

Deleting your account

Request deletion from My account in the app, or on the account deletion page. The club administrator deletes your account and the personal details tied to it: your sign-in, name, email, phone number, and alert settings. Work records you created stay with the club as part of the equipment history. Your name can still appear on a record where it was written in, such as a signature or a note.

Children

Clubhouse IQ is a workplace tool and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. For questions, contact the club's facilities department.
